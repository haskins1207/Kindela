## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Kindela — the operating system for mission-driven nonprofits. *Every donation, grant, and good — connected and visible.*
**What it does:** Kindela unifies donor & grant tracking (inbound *and* outbound), donated-goods inventory, fund accounting, and volunteer management into one source of truth — then turns that truth outward through a public transparency layer that lets donors and the public verify exactly where money and goods go.
**Product category:** Nonprofit operations software / all-in-one nonprofit management platform. Customers search across several "shelves" because no single category contains the whole need: donor management/CRM, nonprofit (fund) accounting, resale/thrift POS, volunteer management, and impact/transparency reporting.
**Product type:** Vertical B2B SaaS (purpose-built for nonprofits).
**Business model:** Tiered, nonprofit-friendly subscription with a genuine free trial and migration help. Starter (~$39–49/mo, small teams getting organized) → Growth (~$119–149/mo, most-chosen, full suite) → Network (custom, larger/multi-program). Annual discount ~20%. No credit card to start.

## Target Audience
**Target companies:** Mission-driven nonprofits trying to organize and run their business operations — from small thrift-store/resale operations to mid- and large children's-mission and community organizations. Budgets ~$250K to several million. **Positioning is broad (all mission-driven nonprofits); resale/donated-goods + outbound-granting depth is available when an org needs the full suite (e.g., Children's Collective of Florida).**
**Decision-makers:** Executive Director / President (primary), Finance/Treasurer, Development/Fundraising, Operations, and the Board.
**Primary use case:** Replace the 5+ disconnected tools a nonprofit duct-tapes together (accounting + donor CRM + POS + grants calendar + volunteer sign-up + spreadsheets) with one connected system — and prove impact to funders and the public.
**Jobs to be done:**
- "Give me one source of truth so I stop re-keying data and carrying the whole picture in my head."
- "Let me answer 'where did every dollar and every donated good go?' instantly — for a donor, a funder, the board, or an auditor."
- "Get my evenings back so I can spend time on the mission, not the machinery."
**Use cases:**
- Logging cash + in-kind gifts, auto-acknowledging, and staying Form 990–ready.
- Tracking inbound grants you're chasing and outbound grants you disburse to partner orgs.
- Routing donated goods to the resale floor *or* to partner nonprofits, with a verifiable audit trail (the Donation-to-Destination Tracer).
- Reconciling restricted vs. unrestricted funds and exporting board decks in seconds.
- Publishing a live, embeddable public transparency report.

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Mission-driven nonprofits run on a patchwork of tools that don't talk to each other, so leaders spend nights re-keying data and can't cleanly prove where money and goods went.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Enterprise nonprofit platforms are priced for huge foundations (too expensive).
- Generic retail POS and accounting tools have no concept of in-kind donations, fund accounting, or routing goods to other nonprofits (too basic / wrong-fit).
- Nothing handles resale store + donor tracking + grants + outbound granting + volunteers + public transparency in one place.
**What it costs them:** Lost staff hours to double entry, lost grant renewals from slow/unclean reporting, untracked inventory at audit, eroded donor trust, burnout.
**Emotional tension:** Overwhelm, financial/compliance anxiety, and a gnawing fear of being "one mistake away from a crisis that hurts the kids she serves."

## Competitive Landscape
*(Deep profiles live in `Kindela/kindela-competitive-landscape.md`; SWOT in `Kindela/kindela-swot.md`.)*
**Direct (all-in-one nonprofit platforms):** Bloomerang, Neon One, Keela, Blackbaud (Raiser's Edge NXT), Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud — broad nonprofit management, but generally no resale/thrift POS, no donated-goods routing to partner orgs, and limited public-facing verifiable transparency; enterprise options are costly/complex.
**Secondary (point tools the patchwork is made of):** QuickBooks / QB for Nonprofits (accounting); DonorPerfect, Little Green Light (donor CRM); Square, Lightspeed, KORONA POS, Epos Now (generic POS); ThriftCart, ConsignPro, SimpleConsign, Ricochet (resale/consignment POS); Zeffy (free fundraising); SignUpGenius (volunteers) — each solves one slice; none unify or connect them.
**Indirect (status quo):** Excel, Google Sheets/Docs, and manual processes — free and familiar, but fragile, unconnected, and impossible to audit or publish credibly.

## Differentiation
**Key differentiators:**
- **Nonprofit-native data model:** in-kind donations, restricted vs. unrestricted funds, Form 990, and **outbound granting** — concepts generic tools don't have.
- **Donated-goods routing + Donation-to-Destination Tracer:** trace each good from intake to resale sale *or* transfer to a partner nonprofit, with a verifiable audit trail. (Unique among both nonprofit CRMs and resale POS.)
- **Public Transparency layer:** a live, embeddable, verifiable impact report — trust as a product feature, not an afterthought.
- **True all-in-one:** replaces ~5 tools (accounting + donor CRM + POS + grants + volunteers) instead of integrating them.
- **Nonprofit-conscious pricing + migration help:** capable depth at a price a $250K org can justify.
**How we do it differently:** One connected source of truth (enter once, right everywhere) with the public-trust layer built in.
**Why that's better:** Leaders get time back; funders and the public can verify impact, and trust becomes the engine of funding.
**Why customers choose us:** It's the only platform built for how a resale-running, grant-giving, mission-driven nonprofit *actually* operates — capable enough for the full suite, simple and affordable enough for a one-person shop.

| "It's either too expensive or too basic." | Nonprofit-conscious tiers from ~$39/mo; transparent pricing; replaces multiple paid subscriptions. |

**Anti-persona:** For-profit retail/thrift businesses; very large foundations needing heavy custom enterprise builds; orgs with zero earned-revenue/in-kind component who only need a pure donor CRM (we can serve them but we're not uniquely differentiated there).

## Switching Dynamics
**Push:** A painful event (failed audit, lost grant renewal, board pressure for better reporting); exhaustion from re-keying across 5 tools.
**Pull:** One system that connects everything + a public page funders trust; "a peer ED said it changed everything."
**Habit:** "It's faster if I just do it myself"; years of data locked in QuickBooks/spreadsheets/CRM; comfort with familiar (if painful) tools.
**Anxiety:** Migration risk, cost, learning curve, "what if it doesn't fit how we work."
