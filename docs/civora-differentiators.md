## Positioning statement

> **For** mission-driven nonprofits — especially those that run a resale or donated-goods operation — **who** are exhausted by stitching together five disconnected tools and can't cleanly prove where their money and goods go, **Civora is** the operating system that connects donors, grants (in and out), donated-goods inventory, fund accounting, and volunteers into one source of truth **and** turns that truth into a public, verifiable transparency report. **Unlike** donor CRMs (no resale/accounting), retail POS (no nonprofit concepts), or enterprise platforms like Blackbaud/Salesforce (priced for huge foundations), **Civora** is the only platform built for how a resale-running nonprofit actually operates — capable enough for the full suite, affordable enough for a one-person shop.

## The five differentiators (ranked by defensibility)

### 1. Donated-goods routing to partner nonprofits — *nobody else does this*
**What:** Trace each donated good from intake to its destination — sold on the resale floor **or** routed to a partner nonprofit — with a verifiable audit trail (the **Donation-to-Destination Tracer**).
**Why it matters:** Every resale/thrift POS assumes goods are *sold*; none handle *redistribution* to other orgs. This is Civora's sharpest, most defensible moat.
**Proof to show:** the Tracer (intake → partner → verified); "the one-eared rabbit went to a child on May 19, and you can prove it."
**Say:** "Goods you can't trace" → "Every donated good, accounted for — sold or given."

### 2. Resale POS + real fund accounting in one system — *never coexist elsewhere*
**What:** A donated-goods POS *and* restricted vs. unrestricted fund accounting with POS-to-bank reconciliation and 990/board-deck exports — in the same tool.
**Why it matters:** Resale tools (ThriftCart, KORONA, Square) have no fund accounting; accounting/CRM tools (QuickBooks, Bloomerang) have no resale POS. Orgs duct-tape them today.
**Proof:** "Books that balance" + "May reconciliation balanced ✓."
**Say:** "Your POS and your books, finally the same system."

### 3. Public, verifiable transparency layer — *trust as a feature*
**What:** A live, embeddable, public impact report donors/funders/press can verify themselves (revenue raised, grants written, goods placed with children).
**Why it matters:** Only Salesforce approaches this — via private-ish portals, at enterprise cost. No one offers a *public, shareable, verifiable* layer. Directly answers the avatar's deepest fear ("could I prove where every dollar went?").
**Proof:** the Public Transparency dashboard + Tracer.
**Say:** "Trust, made visible — anyone can verify it."

### 4. Outbound granting for the mid-market — *Salesforce power without the price*
**What:** Track grants you *disburse* to on-the-ground partners, not just grants you receive.
**Why it matters:** Only Salesforce Nonprofit Cloud does outbound granting natively — at premium per-user pricing requiring a consultant. Bloomerang/Neon/Keela are inbound-only.
**Say:** "Inbound and outbound grants, one calm pipeline."

### 5. True all-in-one at nonprofit-conscious pricing — *replaces ~5 tools*
**What:** Donors + grants + goods + POS + accounting + volunteers + transparency from ~$39/mo with guided migration.
**Why it matters:** Blackbaud (~$3,375/mo+) and Salesforce (per-user + Grantmaking + implementation) price out $250K–$3M orgs; everyone affordable is fragmented.
**Proof:** the Savings Calculator ("Stop paying for five tools").
**Say:** "One system, nonprofit-priced — built-in, not bolted-on."

## Messaging guardrails (from the research)

- **Don't pick a fight with one vendor** — the enemy is *the patchwork* (5 tools + spreadsheets). Name the chaos, not a competitor, in top-of-funnel copy.
- **Be honest about being new.** No invented logos, counts, or badges — the honesty *is* the differentiator for a trust-first buyer. Use "illustrative" labels until verified.
- **Earn the accounting claim.** Don't overstate fund accounting until it's demonstrably audit-grade; the treasurer persona will test it.
- **Keep the resale wedge visible** even under the broadened "all nonprofits" positioning — it's the proof of how deeply Civora understands operations (resale = depth available when needed, e.g., CCOF).
- **Lead with relief/outcomes** ("get your time back for the mission") then back it with the unique mechanism (the Tracer, the public report).
